# /calendar — Calendar & holidays

**Purpose:** One month view of holidays, team leave, your own attendance and work anniversaries.

**Access:** Everyone. Leave shown = your own + people you manage. Holidays: Super Admin adds/removes.

**Features**
- Month grid with today ring, weekly-off shading, holiday pills, leave pills (approved; your pending ones in amber), 🎉 anniversaries, coloured dot for your attendance status
- Mobile: compact dots per day
- Tap a day → details sheet (delete holiday for Super Admin)
- Upcoming holidays list

**Effect elsewhere:** holidays are excluded from working days in attendance %, performance score and streaks, and show in the Celebrations widget.

**Data:** `holidays`, `leave_requests`, `attendance`, `profiles.join_date`.
